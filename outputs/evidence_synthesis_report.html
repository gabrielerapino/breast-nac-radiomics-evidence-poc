01 Research question

Does ultrasound-based radiomics provide incremental predictive value for predicting response to neoadjuvant therapy in breast cancer beyond clinical and conventional ultrasound-based models?

Secondary question: how consistent is performance across imaging timepoints, strategies, model components and validation designs?

High predictive performance does not by itself demonstrate incremental value.

This POC demonstrates a deterministic source-to-report workflow built by an agent using AGENTS.md. Four studies were prespecified; no comprehensive search, screening or automatic meta-analysis was performed.

13 Final synthesis

  • Four prespecified studies; 3 endpoint categories (RECIST, Miller–Payne MHR, pCR). This is not an exhaustive systematic review.
  • Available primary matched contrasts: 4 Demonstrated, 5 Suggestive, 2 Not demonstrated, 4 Indeterminate. Contrasts within a study are correlated and are not independent study counts.
  • Zhou 2024 has a direct clinical+radiomics versus clinical-only baseline validation contrast supported by Figure 5H; it provides evidence within one small internal cohort.
  • Wu 2025 validates multimodal models externally, but longitudinal information, clinical and deep features complicate attribution to radiomics alone; table/prose discrepancies leave effect sizes provisional.
  • Wang 2025 exact validation AUCs and dataset attribution of tests remain unresolved. Yang distinguishes early and delta from baseline; training tests are not borrowed for validation.
  • Poolability: not justified. No joint stratum contains two independent studies with the same endpoint, imaging role, model composition and validation role. The pCR pair uses baseline versus pre+midpoint radiomics; operational pathology and source precision also need review. AUC differences alone cannot supply the within-patient covariance needed to pool incremental effects.
  • 18 REVIEW_REQUIRED checks remain visible. Clinical usefulness beyond discrimination is not established by AUC differences alone.

# External methodological benchmark

Ultrasound-based radiomics for early predicting response to neoadjuvant chemotherapy in patients with breast cancer: a systematic review with meta-analysis (2024), DOI 10.1007/s11547-024-01783-1.

Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11547-024-01783-1
Access: Publisher abstract and subscription preview only; full review not accessible

The review searched four databases through 1 January 2023 and included eight studies. It assessed methods using RQS and QUADAS-2; mean RQS was 12.88 (35.8% of the maximum). Pooled sensitivity was 0.87 (95% CI 0.81–0.92), specificity 0.78 (0.72–0.83). It described potential predictive usefulness while calling for rigorous multicenter prospective confirmation because the evidence was limited and methodologically weak. These are abstract-level findings, not a full-text methodological audit.

The review predates the two 2025 studies and covers a broader evidence set. Its pooled accuracy is not a pooled incremental contrast against clinical/conventional ultrasound models. The four-study POC retains distinct outcomes and imaging roles; it supports cautious interpretation of combined-model performance and does not borrow benchmark numbers for extraction.

This project was exposed to the benchmark previously. The current comparison occurs after the new freeze, but cannot be described as blind or independent. Benchmark findings do not change the primary synthesis.

The inherited project had already consulted the benchmark. The new freeze precedes this run’s benchmark step, but the work is not blind.

15 Limitations of the AI pipeline

  • Protocol-selected four studies; no exhaustive search or screening.
  • Narrative transcription and semantic mappings were curated by the building agent; deterministic source checks do not replace independent clinical appraisal.
  • Source contradictions, missing exact validation metrics and uncertain feature-selection nesting remain.
  • No patient data, model fitting, invented confidence intervals or imputed confusion matrices.
  • Previous benchmark exposure prevents a claim of blind synthesis.

The workflow demonstrates reproducibility of reviewed transcriptions, interpretations and calculations. It is not a general autonomous extractor for arbitrary new articles, and source contradictions cannot be repaired by repeated execution.
